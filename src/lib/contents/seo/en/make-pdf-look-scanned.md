## What is a Make PDF Look Scanned Tool?

**Make PDF Look Scanned** is a free browser-based tool that takes a clean, digital PDF and gives it a realistic scanned or photocopied look. Drop in any PDF, pick a scanner style, and download a PDF with the texture and imperfections of a real scanned or photocopied page.

Everything runs locally using WebAssembly, so your file never leaves your device. There's no upload, no account, and no watermark — just a PDF that looks like it came off a scanner instead of straight out of Word or Google Docs.

---

## Why Make a PDF Look Scanned?

A perfectly clean, digital-native PDF can actually look *out of place* in situations where a scanned document is expected. Common reasons people convert a normal PDF to a scanned-looking one:

- Submitting a document to a government portal, school, or bank that only accepts a scanned copy, not a native digital PDF
- Matching the format of other scanned documents in a shared folder or case file
- Printing and re-submitting a form where a "wet" scanned copy is the norm
- Giving an old or archival document a consistent photocopy look
- Testing how OCR or document-processing software handles scanned input
- Making a digital PDF look handwritten or aged for creative, presentation, or mockup purposes

If you just need to turn a normal PDF into a scanned-style document online, without installing anything, that's exactly what this tool is for.

## Scanner Style & Intensity Options

This tool gives you two simple controls instead of a pile of confusing filters:

- **Scanner Style** — choose the look you want:
  - **B&W Photocopy** — classic black & white photocopier contrast
  - **Grainy Noise** — adds scan grain and texture to an otherwise color page
  - **Vintage Paper** — an aged, old-paper look, useful for a handwritten or archival feel
  - **Modern Color** — a lighter, more subtle scan look that keeps color intact
- **Scanner Effect Intensity** — a 1–5 slider controlling how strong the effect is (default: **Level 2**). Lower levels give a subtle, barely-there scan texture; higher levels give a heavier, more worn photocopy look.

Pick a style first, then adjust the intensity slider until the result matches how "scanned" you want the page to look.

## How to Make a PDF Look Scanned Online

1. Drop your PDF into the upload area, or click to browse.
2. Choose a Scanner Style — B&W Photocopy, Grainy Noise, Vintage Paper, or Modern Color.
3. Adjust the Scanner Effect Intensity slider (1–5) if the default Level 2 isn't strong enough, or is too strong.
4. Download the converted, scanned-look PDF directly. Nothing is stored, and no email or signup is required.

Because everything processes on your own device, there's no daily conversion cap — the tool works the same whether you're converting one page or a full document.

## Common Issues When Making a PDF Look Scanned

**The result looks too clean or too fake.** Try raising the Scanner Effect Intensity above the default Level 2 — a higher level adds more grain and contrast, which reads as more convincingly "scanned."

**The result looks too rough or text is harder to read.** Lower the intensity slider, or switch to **Modern Color** for a lighter, more subtle scan effect that keeps text more legible than B&W Photocopy or Grainy Noise at high intensity.

**I want an aged or handwritten look, not a photocopy look.** Use the **Vintage Paper** style instead of B&W Photocopy — it's built for that older, worn-document feel rather than a crisp office-scanner look.

**I usually do this in Acrobat or with a Python script.** Both work, but take real setup for a one-off file. This tool gives a comparable result in a browser in a few clicks, with no software install or code required.

### What's the difference between the four Scanner Style options?
B&W Photocopy gives a classic black & white contrast look, Grainy Noise adds scan texture while keeping color, Vintage Paper gives an aged or handwritten feel, and Modern Color applies a lighter, more subtle scan effect.

### What does the Scanner Effect Intensity setting do?
It's a 1–5 slider (default Level 2) that controls how strong the scanned effect looks — lower levels are subtle, higher levels give a heavier, more worn photocopy look.

### Can I make a PDF look old or handwritten, not just like a photocopy?
Yes — select the Vintage Paper style, which is designed for an aged, handwritten-adjacent look rather than a standard office photocopy.
