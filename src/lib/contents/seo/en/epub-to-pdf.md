## What is an EPUB to PDF Converter?

**EPUB to PDF Converter** is a free browser-based tool that turns your ebook into a standard PDF without sending the file to a server. Drop in an EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, DJVU, CHM, or LIT file, and download a PDF version — no upload required, no account, no watermark.

Everything runs locally using WebAssembly, so your ebook — purchased, borrowed, or self-authored — never leaves your device. That makes it a fast, private way to get a PDF copy of a book you want to read on a device or app that doesn't support your ebook's original format.

## Why Convert an Ebook to PDF?

EPUB and MOBI are reflowable formats — the text adjusts to fit whatever screen it's on, which is great for e-readers but inconsistent everywhere else. PDF fixes that: it renders identically on any phone, laptop, or printer, and it's the format most work, school, and printing tools expect by default.

Common reasons people convert:

- Reading on a device or app that only supports PDF
- Printing a chapter, or the whole book, with predictable page breaks
- Archiving a personal ebook library in one consistent format
- Sharing a document with someone who doesn't have an ebook reader installed
- Submitting a manuscript or document where PDF is the required format

## Common Issues When Converting

**Formatting looks different from the original ebook.** This is expected. EPUB and MOBI are reflowable; PDF is fixed-layout. The converter lays out the reflowable text onto fixed pages, so page breaks and pagination won't exactly match what you saw in your e-reader app.

**Images or cover art missing.** Rare, but can happen with older or malformed EPUB files exported from unusual tools. Re-exporting the EPUB from its original source app before converting usually resolves it.

**"My file won't convert."** The most common cause is DRM. Ebooks bought through major retailers (Kindle Store, Kobo, Google Play Books) are encrypted by the seller, and no browser-based tool — this one included — can read or convert a DRM-protected file.

### Does converting to PDF keep the original formatting?
Text, images, and chapter structure are preserved. Pagination shifts slightly because EPUB/MOBI are reflowable and PDF is fixed-layout — that's a format difference, not a conversion error.
