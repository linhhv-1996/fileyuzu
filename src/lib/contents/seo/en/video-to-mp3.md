## MP3 Quality: What to Expect

The converter outputs MP3 at the highest practical bitrate (320kbps), so the audio stays as close to the source as MP3 encoding allows. You don't need to pick a bitrate — it's handled automatically.

Keep in mind the ceiling is set by your original video's audio track. If the source audio was recorded or compressed at low quality, converting to MP3 won't add quality back — it just preserves what's there as faithfully as possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this video to MP3 converter really free?

Yes. There's no limit on the number of conversions and no account required.

### Will converting my video reduce the audio quality?

Some quality loss is unavoidable with MP3 since it's a compressed format, but at 320kbps the difference is very hard to hear. If the source video's audio is already low quality, converting won't improve it.

### Is there a file size or duration limit?

Since the conversion happens in your browser rather than on a server, the practical limit is your device's memory and processing power rather than a fixed cap.

### Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. The audio extraction happens entirely on your device. Your file is never sent to a server.
