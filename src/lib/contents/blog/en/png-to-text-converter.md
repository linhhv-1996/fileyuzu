## What Is a PNG to Text Converter?

A PNG to text converter is an OCR tool that reads text inside a `.png` image and turns it into copyable text.

OCR stands for **Optical Character Recognition**. Instead of treating your PNG as a flat image, OCR analyzes the shapes of letters, words, lines, and blocks of text. It then converts the visible text into a digital format you can copy, edit, search, translate, or save.

For example, you may have a PNG image such as:

* a screenshot from an app
* a website capture
* an error message
* a receipt image
* a document screenshot
* a quote image
* a software dialog
* a chart or dashboard with labels

After OCR, the text inside the PNG becomes editable and reusable.

This is useful when you need to copy text from a PNG without manually typing everything again.

PNG and JPG can both work with OCR, but they are often used for different kinds of images.

| Format   | Common Source                                            | OCR Notes                                                             |

## Common PNG OCR Problems

PNG is useful for OCR, but the result still depends on the image quality and layout.

### The Text Is Too Small

Small text can be difficult for OCR, even in PNG images. If possible, zoom in before taking the screenshot or crop around the text area.

### The Background Is Transparent

Some PNG images have transparent backgrounds. If the text color does not contrast clearly with the page or viewer background, OCR may struggle.

### The Text Is Over a Busy Image

Text placed over photos, gradients, patterns, or colorful graphics can be harder to detect. High contrast gives better results.

### The Layout Has Multiple Columns

OCR may read text in the wrong order when the PNG contains columns, tables, sidebars, or complex layouts. Cropping one section at a time can help.

### The Image Was Resized

If a PNG was scaled down before OCR, small letters may become blurred or broken. Use the original image when possible.

## Tips for Better PNG OCR Results

Small changes can improve OCR accuracy a lot.

### Crop Around the Text

Remove unnecessary background, menus, sidebars, or empty space. Cropping helps the OCR engine focus on the text you actually need.

### Use the Original PNG

Avoid using a resized, compressed, or re-exported copy if you have the original PNG. The original file usually keeps text sharper.

### Zoom In Before Taking a Screenshot

If you are capturing text from a web page, app, PDF, or video, zoom in before taking the screenshot. Larger text usually gives better OCR results.

### Keep Strong Contrast

Dark text on a light background, or light text on a dark background, is easier to recognize than low-contrast text.

### Check Numbers and Codes Manually

OCR can confuse similar characters such as `O` and `0`, `I` and `1`, or `B` and `8`. Always proofread important numbers, IDs, prices, dates, and codes.
