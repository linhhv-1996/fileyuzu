## Hi, I'm J.Julian 👋

Welcome to UploadLess! I'm thrilled you're here.

I'm currently working as a Software Engineer at Sepay. Throughout my career in software development, I've often found myself needing simple, quick tools for everyday tasks—things like converting a file format, compressing a video, or comparing text. Instead of searching for these tools every time and navigating through ad-heavy or slow websites, I decided to build my own.

That's how **UploadLess** was born.

## What is UploadLess?

UploadLess is a personal passion project. It's a collection of fast, free, and easy-to-use online utilities designed to make your daily digital tasks simpler. I built this site to be exactly the kind of tool I would want to use: clean, efficient, and respectful of your privacy. 

Everything is designed to run as smoothly and quickly as possible, ensuring your work gets done without unnecessary waiting.

## The Vision

My goal is to keep expanding UploadLess with more handy tools as I encounter new needs (or as you suggest them!). I want this to be a reliable toolkit you can bookmark and turn to whenever you need a quick fix, without the hassle.

If you find UploadLess helpful, or if you have ideas for new tools, feel free to drop me a message on the Contact page. I'm always open to feedback!

Thanks for stopping by and using UploadLess.
